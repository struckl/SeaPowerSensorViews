0.2.2: fixes the lag and freeze when zooming in on the map with the sensor lines on.
Zoomed in, the sensor lines and bearing wedges were drawn in full length far past the edge of the screen, which made the game crawl and at high zoom freeze. They are now cut to the visible map before they are drawn. Measured on 0.8.7: at zoom 13 from about 31-61 fps with half-second stutters to about 72-74 fps; at zoom 17 the old version froze the game, now about 74-76 fps.
Also: the lines are no longer rebuilt every second while the game is paused. Thanks to everyone who reported it.
